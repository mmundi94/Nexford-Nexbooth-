How Nexbooth works

  1. Decorate. Pick an alpaca, props, words, a frame, and a filter from the panel.
  2. Arrange. Drag a sticker to move it. Drag the round corner handle to resize and rotate. On a phone, pinch with two fingers.
  3. Snap. Press the big button (or the space bar). Choose "Strip of 4" for a classic photo strip.
  4. Share. Download your photo and post it for the team.

Everything happens in your browser. No photos are uploaded or stored.
